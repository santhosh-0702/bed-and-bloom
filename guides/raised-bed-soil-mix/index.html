Building soil

How much soil does a raised bed need?

The answer is just volume—but a few small measuring choices make a big difference at checkout.

ADVERTISEMENT · available placement

The formula

Multiply the bed’s inside length by its inside width. Multiply that area by the soil depth in inches, then divide by 12 to convert the depth to feet.

Length (ft) × width (ft) × depth (in) ÷ 12 = cubic feet

For example, an 8 × 4 ft bed filled 12 inches deep needs 32 cubic feet. Use the soil calculator to do the conversion to bags and cubic yards.

Leave a little room

Measure the depth to where you want the soil to finish, not necessarily the full height of the lumber. Leaving about an inch below the rim gives you room to water without washing soil over the edge.

Buying in bags or bulk

Bag sizes vary, so read the label. Bulk suppliers typically sell by the cubic yard: divide cubic feet by 27. For a large bed, compare the delivered bulk price with the total cost of bags before ordering.
