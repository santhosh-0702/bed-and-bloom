Mulching

How deep should mulch be?

A good mulch layer protects the soil without turning into a wet blanket around your plants.

A useful starting point

For most garden beds, aim for 2 to 3 inches. Use a thinner layer around small seedlings so their stems stay clear. Paths often need 3 to 4 inches, especially where mulch will compact under foot traffic.

Keep trunks and stems clear

Pull mulch back from plant stems and tree trunks. A small bare ring keeps moisture from staying against the bark and makes it easier to spot problems.

Calculate what to buy

Measure the area in feet and choose a depth in inches. The mulch calculator converts that into cubic yards and 2-cubic-foot bags. Buy slightly more for an irregular border; it is easier to spread extra than to match a second load.
