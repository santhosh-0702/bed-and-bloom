How to use this estimate

Measure the inside of the bed, not the outside. If your bed has multiple layers or a tapered shape, calculate each section separately and add the results.

Bag sizes vary, so check the label before buying. The bag estimate here uses 1.5 cubic feet, a common retail size. For bulk soil, use the cubic-yard number and ask the supplier how much settling allowance they recommend.

What should go in a raised bed?

Use a quality soil designed for raised beds, or blend topsoil with finished compost. Avoid filling a whole bed with pure compost; it can shrink and hold more water than roots need.
