Choosing a mulch depth

Two to three inches is a practical starting point for most annual beds. Keep mulch a few inches away from plant stems and tree trunks so moisture does not sit against the bark.

For a larger project, bulk mulch is usually cheaper than bags. The cubic-yard result is the number to give a landscape supplier.
