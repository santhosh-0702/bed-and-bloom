About

Garden math for real backyards.

Bed & Bloom is a small, independent collection of calculators and guides for home gardeners. The aim is simple: answer the practical question in front of you, explain the assumptions, and get out of the way.

How this site is supported

This site may eventually use clearly labeled display advertising, affiliate links to relevant garden supplies, and optional premium planning features. The basic tools will remain free.

A note on estimates

Results are estimates, not guarantees. Check product labels, local growing conditions, and advice from your local extension service for decisions that depend on your specific climate or soil.
